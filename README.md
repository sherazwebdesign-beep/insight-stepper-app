# Insight Stepper App

Redesign the website for LR Transformational Coaching (https://lrtransformationalcoaching.com/) with an unique, fresh, and modern look featuring fluid, welcoming animations, and a warm organic palette (soft stone, warm taupe, linen, and serene sage/earthen accents).

Website Content and Sections to Include:
1. Navigation & Header:
   - Elegant logo/monogram ("LR Transformational Coaching")
   - Smooth navigation links: About, Transform Model™, Comparison, Programme, Book Call
   - Quick action CTA: "Book Discovery Call"

2. Hero Section:
   - Headline: "Where clarity drives TRANSFORMATION"
   - Subhead: Helping professionals navigate life transitions, overcome self-doubt, and create meaningful, lasting change.
   - Trust indicators: "ICF Professional Certified Coach", "16+ Years Corporate Finance Experience", "Health & Fitness Background"
   - Interactive pain-point selector or gentle pill toggles ("Career Crossroads", "Executive Burnout", "Identity & Confidence Shift")
   - Primary and secondary CTAs ("Book 30-Min Discovery Call", "Explore the Journey")

3. The TRANSFORM MODEL™ (Interactive Feature):
   - Interactive 5-stage stepper or tabbed card system with smooth animated transitions:
     - Stage 1: Awareness (Uncover patterns, beliefs, and habits shaping current results)
     - Stage 2: Vision (Define what you want next: goals, identity, and intentional alignment)
     - Stage 3: Mindset Shift (Challenge old narratives, reframe limiting beliefs, build self-trust)
     - Stage 4: Aligned Action (Transform insight into practical steps aligned with core values)
     - Stage 5: Sustainable Growth (Tools and strategies to maintain lifelong momentum)

4. Differentiator / Comparison Deck:
   - Interactive comparison cards explaining how Transformational Coaching differs from:
     - Therapy (Focuses on healing the past vs. present and future self)
     - Mentoring (Mentors share experience vs. uncovering your own answers)
     - Business Coaching (Targets strategy vs. inside-out identity and beliefs)

5. Coach Story & Philosophy:
   - Editorial bio section highlighting the founder's 16+ year career in major financial institutions, facing burnout and career transitions, and moving into accredited coaching.
   - Philosophy highlight: "Transformation is not about becoming someone else. It's about becoming more of who you truly are."

6. Coaching Programmes & Pricing:
   - The 6-Month Transformational Coaching Journey card:
     - 6 x 60-minute one-to-one coaching sessions (monthly)
     - Ongoing accountability & support between sessions
     - Clear investment: £90 per session equivalent (£540 programme), with £95 additional sessions
   - Complimentary 30-Minute Discovery Call card

7. Interactive Discovery Call Booking & Contact Form:
   - Seamless booking step modal/form allowing visitors to select a topic/goal, pick a preferred time slot, and submit details with a warm confirmation state.

8. Polish & Animations:
   - Fluid entrance animations, soft hover states, subtle ambient gradients, and crisp responsive layout on mobile and desktop.
